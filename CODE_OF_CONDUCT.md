# Community code of conduct

ImportSpy welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience.
Everyone participating in issues, pull requests, reviews, documentation, or
project discussions is expected to treat others with respect.

Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and give actionable feedback.
Respect people's time, boundaries, names, and privacy. Accept corrections and
focus on improving the work together.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
personal insults, and publication of another person's private information are
not acceptable. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern.

Report concerns privately to maintainer Luca Atella at **info@atellaluca.com**.
Include relevant links and context, and avoid sharing unnecessary personal
information. Reports will be handled with discretion; information will be shared
only as needed to investigate and respond.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, ask for changes in
behavior, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently. Responses should
reflect the conduct and its impact, and the person affected should receive an
explanation when practical. These expectations apply to maintainers as well as
contributors and to people representing the project in public spaces.
